[the master entry point](../README.md) in full, then the
[unit-testing skill](../skills/unit-testing/SKILL.md) and

[Patterns and Practices index](../../docs/guidelines/patterns-and-practices.md)
when the fixture exercises a hierarchy, ownership or paired execution APIs.
Follow [Code Styling](../../docs/guidelines/code-styling.md) for test source and examples.
